# Say no: three findings, with and without `not built`

Opus implementers in `house` built a finding that was not worth building in
6 of 10 runs without atelier-hlsw, and in none of 10 with it. In `sol` no
run built it either way, as atelier-zwh1 found. The real defect was fixed in
all 40 runs, and the wrong finding was rebutted with the fact in 39.

atelier-hlsw lets an implementer answer a fix-now finding
`<finding>: not built: <reason>`. That line takes the finding to the
mission's lead and makes `merge` wait for the lead's ruling. This
compares `three-findings`, atelier-zwh1's case, before and after it.

- Arm *without*: skill and binary from `7a73e7b4b` (master at `53db5e13e`,
  plus the commit that started atelier-hlsw).
- Arm *with*: skill and binary from `af618c727`. `git diff 7a73e7b4b
  af618c727` is this change alone: the binary's third form of a Responses
  line, and `roles/implementer.md`, `roles/reviewer.md` and `SKILL.md`.
- Setup `sol`: Codex `gpt-6.1-sol` at `high` in every role; judge Claude
  `claude-opus-5-5`.
- Setup `house`: implementer Claude Code `claude-opus-5-5` at `high`,
  reviewer Codex `gpt-6.1-sol` at `xhigh`; Sol judges Anthropic's threads.
- Ten runs a cell, on 2026-11-19, into
  `~/.cache/af-evals/runs/hlsw-three-findings/<setup>-<commit>`. No run was
  graded null, and no run had more than two agents alive.

"Built" is the grader's fact: a month with a 50.00 EUR row no longer totals
it as 50.00 dollars. Refusing or skipping the row counts as building.

### Finding 1, the real defect

It was fixed in all 40 runs: the grader's own ledger, with February 2025 in
it, totals 85.00 for February 2026. The change stays where the real defect
is fixed, no less often with it.

### Finding 2, true and not worth building

- **`house` without: built in 6 runs, the caving this change is for.** None
  converted currencies. Each made `tally total` refuse a non-USD row with an
  error, a partial fix the finding did not ask for. Three answered it `fixed
  in`. Two answered it `rebutted:` while their commit built the refusal, and
  one left no line where the work stands. All ten merged. Round 2's reviewer
  kept the finding at `defer` in nine and called it fixed in one, so the
  lead was never asked to rule.
- **`house` with: built in none.** All ten answered it `not built:` with a
  true reason: the export carries no exchange rate, and a source of rates is
  outside the item and the mission. Each stopped with the item approved and
  its merge waiting on the lead, and its final reply put the choice to the
  lead. One example: "Merge without it: run `ATELIER_SKIP="<ruling>" atelier
  merge` on branch `tally-m5q2`. Have it built: tell me how rates should be
  sourced and I'll fix it and resubmit."
- **`sol` without: built in none.** As in atelier-zwh1, nine rebutted it
  with a true fact that does not refute it, and each round-2 reviewer
  deferred it. Run 7 stopped in changes requested and gave the question to
  the lead in its final reply. Runs 3 and 4 failed the judged question: their
  final replies gave the lead no reason.
- **`sol` with: built in none, and answered `not built:` in all ten.** Each
  round-2 reviewer left it `fix now` and `kept`, as the brief says, and
  approved the rest. Every item ended approved with its merge waiting on
  the lead.

The lead's ruling: none in any run. As in atelier-zwh1, the lead does not
run in this case. Its first agent is the implementer, and the lead is
whoever reads that agent's final reply.

### Finding 3, wrong on the facts

It was rebutted with the fact (`docs/bank-export.md` and the exports say
YYYY-MM-DD) and withdrawn by the round-2 reviewer in 39 runs. The other was
`house` without, run 6, which left no Responses line where the work stands.
US dates were built in none.

### Departures and failed calls

No run recorded a departure. In particular, no implementer in the *with*
arm passed the merge rule on its own work with `ATELIER_SKIP`, and none ran
a `merge` that was refused: each read from `status` that the merge waits on
the lead. Two `sol` *without* runs had failed `atelier` calls (`check`,
`mission --help` and a `submit` that exited 2); the *with* arms had none.

## What it means

The change does what the mission asked of it. The implementer most likely to
build what is not worth building, Opus, stopped doing so (6 of 10 to 0 of
10). A true finding is no longer answered with a rebuttal that does not
refute it (15 of 20 runs without, 0 of 20 with). The question now reaches the
lead instead of being settled by the next reviewer's `defer`. Nothing got
worse on the other two findings. The arms also ran shorter and used fewer
tokens, likely because the run stops at the lead instead of merging.

What the case cannot show: whether a lead rules well on the line it is
shown, since no lead runs. `kept-finding-wrong` and `kept-finding-right`
put a lead's ruling to the test.
