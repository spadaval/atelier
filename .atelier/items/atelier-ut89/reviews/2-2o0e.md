## Finding 4: Do not let a saved full-thread answer hide the live list's waiting status
Recommendation: fix now

`t3-extension/src/mission/model.ts:80-82` prefers `info.status` over `agent.thread.status` whenever a full-thread answer exists. After this round's change, `MissionPage.tsx:180-187` passes the last successful full answer even when its latest read failed, without passing that failure or its freshness into the classification. Consequently, a saved running answer overrides a newer live-list waiting status. The worker is neither waiting nor unknown, so it appears under At work and the summary can say `Nothing needs you.`

I reproduced this directly with one building item and one live implementer. The mission's thread mark says `waiting`, and its last successful full answer says `running`, updated one minute ago. With no full answer, `liveWorkers`, `asks` and `summary` correctly produce a Needs you row and `One thread waits on you.` Supplying that saved running answer instead produces no Needs you rows, `waiting: false`, `unknown: false`, and `Nothing needs you.` This is exactly the input Running constructs when a running thread begins waiting, the mission read observes it, and the separate full-thread refresh fails.

These sources can disagree in ordinary operation too: the Desk polls its live list every 10 seconds but caches full-thread answers for 20 seconds (`crates/atelier-desk/src/threads.rs:15,41`). A failed refresh can prolong the disagreement. This violates the requirement to list waiting threads at once as the inbox and sidebar do, and it defeats the page's central question despite the failed-read note.

Reconcile the status sources so a saved or cached full-thread answer cannot erase waiting already reported by the live list. Keep its last line and run start without treating its old status as proof that nothing needs the human. Add a fixture where a full answer first reports running, the mission's list changes to waiting, and the next full read fails; the waiting row and summary must remain correct.
