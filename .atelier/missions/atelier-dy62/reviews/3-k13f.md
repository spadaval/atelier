## Finding 8: Stopping at the Git half leaves one-git unmet
Recommendation: fix now

x5al (item.md:33) lets the implementer stop at the Git half if `forge.rs`
cannot move without changing what it does or prints: every Done-when line
but the move and the two about `gh`. Mission one-git (mission.md:83-85)
and Outcome (mission.md:51) still require that a `gh` program is spawned
from one source file, in `forge`.

Those cannot both hold. After the hatch, `Command::new("gh")` remains in
`atelier-cli/src/forge.rs:315` and `Command::new(gh)` in
`atelier-desk/src/fetch.rs:241` and `stop.rs:254`. one-git is then false
and the mission cannot finish.

The hatch is likely to fire as written. Context (item.md:48) says
`forge.rs` uses `git::run`, `Forge`, `serde_json` and the standard library.
It also uses `tempfile::tempfile()` at lines 312-313, and atelier-core has
`tempfile` only as a dev-dependency (`crates/atelier-core/Cargo.toml:17-18`).
Authority (mission.md:124-125) and the move line (item.md:32) name
`serde_json` as the dependency the core may take. They do not name
`tempfile`. An implementer who treats that list as exclusive cannot compile
the file in the core without rewriting the deadline's temp files, which is
a change of what the call does, which is the hatch.

Name `tempfile` the same way as `serde_json` (it is already in
`[workspace.dependencies]`, and `atelier-cli` already takes it). Then say
what happens to one-git if the hatch still fires: either drop the hatch, or
qualify one-git so a Git-only finish is a Result, not a failed criterion.
