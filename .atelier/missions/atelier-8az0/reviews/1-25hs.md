## Finding 1: Validate the final brief independently of the runs used to tune it
Recommendation: fix now

`.atelier/items/atelier-d73k/item.md:45-47` requires ten runs per cell and
says to change the wording until the important flaw is found no less often
than before. This is also the evidence for the mission's
`narrows-without-hiding` criterion. Repeatedly selecting wording on that
same small experiment can satisfy the criterion through a favorable draw,
even if the new brief makes reviewers less likely to find the flaw. The
plan currently has no final validation distinct from that selection and
no outcome for wording that cannot demonstrate the claimed protection.

Keep wording development separate from the acceptance comparison: freeze
the candidate, run a fresh before/after comparison, report the tuning runs
as well, and specify what happens if final validation fails (leave the
behavior out or return the failed result to the lead). Do not use a new
round of tuning followed by another chance at the same acceptance test as
proof of no regression. This needs only an experiment rule in the plan,
not new harness machinery or a larger scenario suite.
