## Description

Preflight-rejected transition attempts currently write canonical `transition_blocked` activity even though no workflow action or status mutation occurred. When a transition requires `git.worktree_clean`, that generated sidecar can make the next retry fail after the operator has resolved the original blocker. Reproduced during atelier-411x epic close: the initial close was blocked by uncommitted validation evidence; its generated activity then appeared as the sole dirty entry on subsequent retries. Treat rejected commands as diagnostics rather than durable issue history. Preserve read compatibility for existing historical sidecars, but stop producing new `transition_blocked` activity for unavailable or validator-blocked transitions.

## Outcome

`atelier issue transition <id> <transition>` reports unavailable transitions and failed preflight requirements without changing canonical issue or activity records. Retrying after the reported blocker is resolved is not blocked by activity generated by the prior attempt. Successful transitions continue to record canonical `transition_applied` activity, and existing historical `transition_blocked` sidecars remain readable.
