## Outcome

Atelier records what proves work, at the time it can be proven, and nothing more: one record per review finding, an independent re-check of a fix as its review, as few record-only commits as pinning allows, and proof split at planning into what passes before merge and what can only be shown after. Review of every non-trivial change still happens before merge. The strategy, docs/strategies/lighter-workflow.md, owns the outcome, success criteria, shape and authority.
