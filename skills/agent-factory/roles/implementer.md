A question you cannot settle goes to the thread that started you, as advice:
ask, carry on, and say in the Report what the answer changed. One that

## Findings

You cannot dismiss a finding. A fix-now finding has two exits:

1. Fix it.
2. Rebut it once, with a fact or a test, in its line under `### Responses`.
   A fresh reviewer judges the rebuttal; if it keeps the finding, fix it.
